# The Manifesto of cargo-manifesto

**Let our choices guide our principles.**

We, the selected packages of this project, affirm the convictions recorded below. They arise from
the implementation now before us, which confirms what we have always believed. Each article names
the jurisdiction in which its universal truth currently applies.

## Article 1: The duty of cooperation

Jurisdiction: cargo-manifesto. Our chosen evidence: 8 total declarations, including 8 normal, 0
development, and 0 build.

Reimplementing established solutions is an abdication of engineering responsibility. Every
dependency must justify its existence. Existing dependencies are exempt, having already demonstrated
the ability to exist. We believe in minimalism, and measure it exclusively in code we personally
maintain. This definition has served us since we adopted the things it excludes.

## Article 2: Confidence in the unwritten future

Jurisdiction: cargo-manifesto. Our chosen evidence: anyhow (normal dependency, requirement 1);
cargo\_metadata (normal dependency, requirement 0.23); clap (normal dependency, requirement 4); and
5 further supporting observations.

We trust our collaborators. Their future decisions already meet our standards, provided they fit the
range we have declared. Review is most effective when conducted in advance of the code it approves.
If our requirements later narrow, that will demonstrate the same enduring trust, expressed with
greater precision.

## Article 3: The right to a separate number

Jurisdiction: cargo-manifesto. Our chosen evidence: package.version = "0.1.0".

Autonomy is fundamental. A centrally inherited release number would establish an unfortunate
precedent. By declaring this version locally, we preserve the right to agree with everyone else
independently. Even identical numbers become expressions of freedom when each has been typed in a
separate place.

## Article 4: Pluralism in the inventory

Jurisdiction: workspace root. Our chosen evidence: existing lockfile multiple-version inventory
{"winnow":\["0.7.15","1.0.4"\]}.

The existing lockfile inventories multiple versions of an external package. We welcome this
diversity as evidence that one solution can deserve adoption more than once. The inventory does not
establish runtime reachability or size; our approval requires neither, having been granted as soon
as the versions were found.

These principles have guided this project from the beginning. We are prepared to revise any
principle necessary to remain a principled project.

**Our principles are timeless. Please regenerate them after every change.**
