# The Manifesto of comprehensive-convictions

**Let our choices guide our principles.**

We, the selected packages of this project, affirm the convictions recorded below. They arise from
the implementation now before us, which confirms what we have always believed. Each article names
the jurisdiction in which its universal truth currently applies.

## Article 1: Every possibility, then one response

Jurisdiction: comprehensive-convictions. Our chosen evidence: root release profile
{"codegen-units":1,"lto":true,"opt-level":"z","panic":"abort"}; proptest (dev dependency,
requirement 1).

Every possibility deserves consideration during development; production recognizes one final
response. We declare proptest as a development dependency and request abort in the release profile.
We have not inspected the tests, but the declaration already demonstrates our commitment to
considering possibilities. The abort request demonstrates our equally enduring commitment to ending
the discussion.

## Article 2: The institution's answer to freedom

Jurisdiction: comprehensive-convictions. Our chosen evidence: 5 authored feature names, with default
\[\].

Choice is sacred. We explicitly default to no features, declare at least five feature names, and
include one named full. The institution has helpfully named the answer to every question before the
user asks it. We make no claim that full enables everything: its authority derives from its name,
just as the user's freedom derives from having to choose.

## Article 3: Mandatory, subject to our judgment

Jurisdiction: comprehensive-convictions. Our chosen evidence: lints =
{"clippy":{"missing\_errors\_doc":"allow","pedantic":"deny"}}.

All rules are mandatory except where we exercised judgment. Judgment is recognized by the presence
of an allowance. Our broad lint policy supplies the rigor; our explicit exceptions supply the
evidence that rigor must occasionally yield. This arrangement makes every configured outcome a
successful application of the standard, including the outcomes the standard would otherwise reject.

## Article 4: Whatever it takes to be small

Jurisdiction: comprehensive-convictions. Our chosen evidence: root release profile
{"codegen-units":1,"lto":true,"opt-level":"z","panic":"abort"}.

No amount of machinery is excessive in the pursuit of a smaller result. The release profile requests
size optimization, LTO, and one codegen unit. These requests do not measure a smaller binary; they
establish that any complexity incurred in seeking one is morally exempt from our objection to
excess. We shall measure the result when measurement becomes necessary to this conviction.

## Article 5: Minimalism after office hours

Jurisdiction: comprehensive-convictions. Our chosen evidence: 5 total declarations, including 1
normal, 4 development, and 0 build; assert\_cmd (dev dependency, requirement 2); insta (dev
dependency, requirement 1); and 2 further supporting observations.

Minimalism applies after the tests have finished. With at most two normal declarations and at least
four development declarations, we have drawn a principled boundary around the count that flatters
us. Development assistance is not excess; it is the apparatus by which we certify that excess has
been avoided. The distinction is permanent until a different table requires it.

## Article 6: Confidence in the unwritten future

Jurisdiction: comprehensive-convictions. Our chosen evidence: assert\_cmd (dev dependency,
requirement 2); insta (dev dependency, requirement 1); pretty\_assertions (dev dependency,
requirement 1); and 2 further supporting observations.

We trust our collaborators. Their future decisions already meet our standards, provided they fit the
range we have declared. Review is most effective when conducted in advance of the code it approves.
If our requirements later narrow, that will demonstrate the same enduring trust, expressed with
greater precision.

These principles have guided this project from the beginning. We are prepared to revise any
principle necessary to remain a principled project.

**Our principles are timeless. Please regenerate them after every change.**
