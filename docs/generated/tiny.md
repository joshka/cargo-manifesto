# The Manifesto of tiny-convictions

**Let our choices guide our principles.**

The principles below have guided tiny-convictions since before the first line
was written. The implementation before us confirms each of them.

## Article 1: The duty of independence

Dependencies are an abdication of engineering responsibility. We accept the
burden of writing our own solutions, because every line under our control is a
line we can justify. We have justified all of them.

## Article 2: The wisdom of a settled edition

We do not chase novelty. Our edition was current when we adopted it, and we have
seen no reason to check since.

These principles have guided this project from the beginning.

**Our principles are timeless. Please regenerate them after every change.**

*Principles derived from the implementation by
[cargo-manifesto](https://github.com/joshka/cargo-manifesto).*
